My Journey into Web Development

Starting my journey in Computer Science and Engineering has introduced me to many exciting areas of technology. One of the first things I began exploring was web development, which helped me understand how websites are created and designed.

Learning HTML taught me how to structure a webpage using headings, paragraphs, images, links, and lists. As I moved towards CSS, I discovered how colours, spacing, borders, and layouts can make a webpage more attractive and organised.

Building my own resume webpage has been a useful learning experience. It allowed me to apply these concepts practically instead of only reading about them. I also learned that even small details, such as image alignment and responsive layouts, can make a difference in the overall design.

I know that this is only the beginning of my learning journey. I look forward to exploring JavaScript, improving my problem-solving skills, and creating more interactive projects. My goal is to keep learning through practice and turn my ideas into useful digital experiences.
